HELPFUL PHRASES FOR FORM 2 EMAIL REPLIES

1. RESPONDING
  • Thanks for asking me.
  • Great to hear from you!
  • I'm happy to help you out.
2. CHOOSING / ADVISING
  • I think you should ...
  • In my opinion, you should ...
  • My recommendation is ...
3. REASONS & ELABORATION
  • First, [Reason 1] + [Elaboration]
  • Also, [Reason 2] + [Example]
  • Another reason is [Reason 3] + [Detail]
